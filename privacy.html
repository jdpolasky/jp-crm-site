JP CRM privacy policy

Last updated October 15, 2026.

JP CRM is a personal app with a single user: its owner. It runs only on the owner's own computer.

What it accesses

  • Gmail message headers (read-only, gmail.metadata scope): sender, recipients, date, subject and labels. It cannot read message bodies or attachments, and it cannot send, change or delete mail.
  • Google Calendar events (read-only, calendar.readonly scope): title, time, attendees and whether the owner accepted.

How it is used

Headers and events that involve people already in the owner's contact list are turned into short "who, when, subject" entries in a database on the owner's computer. Everything else is discarded as soon as it is read.

Where it is kept

Only on the owner's own computer, with one exception: when the owner asks his own AI assistant (Anthropic's Claude) about his contacts, the names, dates and subject lines it needs to answer are passed to it. Nothing else is sent to any server other than Google's own APIs, and nothing is sold, shared, or used for advertising.

Google API Services User Data Policy

JP CRM's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access and deleting data

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the app's data folder on the owner's computer deletes everything it stored.
